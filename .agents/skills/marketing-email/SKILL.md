# Marketing — Email

Email is the highest-ROI owned channel a bootstrapped venture has — owned, not
rented. Any tool with automated flows works — Klaviyo, Mailchimp, or your platform's
built-in tool. Build the automated flows first; they earn money while the founder sleeps.
For a non-store business, swap the cart flows below for sign-up, trial, and enquiry
follow-ups.

## UK consent rules — get this right first
- PECR governs marketing email, and PECR fines now match UK GDPR levels. This is not a
  technicality.
- **Soft opt-in:** you may email existing customers about similar products if they were
  given a clear opt-out at the point of sale and in every message.
- For non-customers (a newsletter list), get explicit consent. No pre-ticked boxes.
- Every email needs a working unsubscribe link and a genuine sender identity.
See `@docs/UK-LEGAL-TAX.md`.

## The core automated flows — build these in order
1. **Order confirmation & shipping** — transactional; sets expectations, cuts support tickets.
2. **Welcome flow** — for new subscribers; introduce the brand, set the relationship.
3. **Abandoned cart** — the highest-revenue flow; recover sales already nearly made.
4. **Browse abandonment** — for engaged visitors who did not add to cart.
5. **Post-purchase** — review request, replenishment reminder, considered cross-sell.
6. **Win-back** — re-engage lapsed customers before they are gone for good.

## Campaigns vs flows
- **Flows** are automated and behaviour-triggered — set up once, always on. The priority.
- **Campaigns** are one-off sends (launches, offers). Useful, but flows do the heavy lifting.

## Method
1. Map the flows above to the venture; build the abandoned-cart flow first.
2. Segment — never blast the whole list. Engaged, lapsed, and customer segments differ.
3. Write copy with the `copywriter-brand-voice` subagent; A/B test subject lines.
4. Track revenue per recipient, not just open rate. Open rate is vanity; revenue is the metric.

## Automate first, automate last
Automate order confirmations, shipping notices, and abandoned-cart now. Leave customer
service replies and pricing changes manual until the rules are proven — see the
`ECOM-OPS` doc in the ecommerce pack (`packs/ecommerce/docs/ECOM-OPS.md`), if installed.
