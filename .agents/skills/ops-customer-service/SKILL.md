# Operations — Customer Service

For a live venture, customer service is retention and brand — not a cost centre. Early on
the founder does it personally, and that is also free customer research.

## Tooling by stage
- **Start:** a shared Gmail inbox, or a Help Scout / Front trial. Do not over-tool.
- **Self-hosted free option:** Chatwoot, if the founder wants to host it.
- **Scale-up:** Gorgias (~£50/month), once tight integration with your store platform is
  worth it — roughly the 100+ tickets-a-month mark.
- Phase-gate the upgrade: move tools when ticket volume genuinely demands it, not before.

## Running it well
1. Set a response-time expectation and meet it. A clear "within one working day" beats a
   silent promise of instant replies.
2. Templates for common questions — but always personalise the first line.
3. Log every recurring issue. Repeated questions are a product, copy, or expectations
   problem to fix at the source.
4. Treat complaints as data — and as UK consumer-law signals (see the `ops-returns` skill,
   in the physical pack, if installed).

## Automate first, automate last
Automate order and shipping notifications now (your email tool's automated flows). Keep actual customer-service
replies manual far longer than feels efficient — a wrong automated reply damages the brand
in a way an order confirmation never will.

## The metric
First-response time and resolution rate. But the real signal is whether the same issue keeps
arriving — if it does, fix the cause, do not just answer faster.
