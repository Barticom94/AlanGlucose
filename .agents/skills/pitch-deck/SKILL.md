# Pitch Deck

A pitch deck is mostly a Phase 5 artefact. If the venture has not cleared its earlier gates,
the most honest pitch deck is "not yet". For the build, spawn the `pitch-deck-builder` subagent.

## Before drafting
Read `state/project_brief.md`, `state/financials.md`, `state/progress.md`, and `research/`.
The deck reflects evidence, not hope. If traction is thin, show validation evidence
instead — and say so.

## Standard structure — one message per slide
1. Problem — the pain, in the customer's words.
2. Solution — how you remove it.
3. Why now — what changed to make this possible.
4. Market — TAM/SAM/SOM, built bottoms-up (use `tam-sam-som`).
5. Product — show it, do not describe it.
6. Traction — real numbers from `state/` only.
7. Business model — how money is made; the unit economics.
8. Go-to-market — the channel and its CAC.
9. Competition — honest; "no competitors" loses the room.
10. Team — why this founder, for this market.
11. Financials — the model, three cases.
12. The ask — how much, at what, and what it buys.

Each slide's title states the takeaway, not the topic.

## UK SEIS framing
If raising via SEIS: 50% income tax relief for investors, £200,000 investor cap, £500,000
company cap, company under 3 years trading, gross assets ≤ £350k, fewer than 25 full-time
employees. Directors can invest under SEIS. Verify every limit against gov.uk and
`@docs/UK-FUNDING.md` — they change. Advance assurance takes roughly 4-6 weeks.

## The honesty rule
Never invent traction, customers, or revenue. A fabricated number ends the raise the moment
a single investor checks it — and sophisticated investors check.
