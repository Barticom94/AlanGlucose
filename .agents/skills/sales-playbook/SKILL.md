# Sales Playbook

Early sales are the founder's job and a continuation of customer discovery — every sales
conversation is also evidence. This skill builds a repeatable process for the first ~50
customers.

## First, know the motion
- **Self-serve** (low price, B2C — an online store or a SaaS sign-up) — the "sale" is the
  landing page, the product or pricing page, and the checkout or sign-up. Optimise those; see the marketing skills.
- **Founder-led** (higher price, B2B, a considered purchase) — direct conversations. The
  playbook below applies.

## The founder-led process
1. **Source** — a named list of target customers, not "anyone who might want it".
2. **Reach** — a specific, personal first contact. No spray-and-pray; the UK soft opt-in and
   PECR rules apply to email (see `@docs/UK-LEGAL-TAX.md`).
3. **Discovery** — apply The Mom Test even while selling: their problem first, your product
   second.
4. **Demonstrate** — show the outcome against their actual situation, not a generic demo.
5. **Handle objections** — log every objection. A recurring objection is product or
   positioning feedback, not just a hurdle.
6. **Close** — ask for the commitment plainly: the order, the deposit, the start date. Then
   stay silent and let them answer.
7. **Record** — log the outcome and objections to `research/` and `tasks/`.

## Metrics to track
Conversations → qualified → proposals → closed. Watch the conversion at each step; the
weakest step is where to focus. Track time-to-close and average order value.

## Anti-patterns
- Discounting to close trains customers to wait for discounts and signals low value.
- A "yes, later" with no date is a no. Record it as a no.
- If the same objection kills 5 deals, stop selling and fix the product or the price.
