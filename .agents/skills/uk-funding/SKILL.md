# UK Funding

UK business support is dense and underused — national schemes plus a regional layer that
varies by where the founder is based. Match the venture's stage and structure to the right
programme. Full directory with eligibility and links: `@docs/UK-FUNDING.md`.

## Match funding to phase
- **Phase 0-1 (validating):** mostly free support — the founder's regional Growth Hub,
  bank-run accelerators (NatWest Accelerator, Barclays Eagle Labs), Help to Grow: Management.
- **Phase 2 (MVP):** Start Up Loans (up to £25k personal, fixed 6% APR, 12 months free
  mentoring); regional grants via the local Growth Hub or Combined Authority.
- **Phase 3-4 (traction/growth):** British Business Bank regional investment funds (SME
  loans and equity); regional grant challenges.
- **Phase 5 (scale/raise):** SEIS/EIS equity, angel groups, SEIS funds.

## Use the founder's region
Regional support is real but local. Establish where the founder is based, then research
**their** region's Growth Hub, Combined Authority funds, and any British Business Bank
regional fund covering them. Do not assume — find it. The gov.uk Business Finance Support
finder (gov.uk/business-finance-support) is the starting point.

## SEIS / EIS at a glance
- **SEIS** — 50% income tax relief for investors, £200k investor cap, £500k company cap;
  company under 3 years trading, gross assets ≤ £350k, under 25 FTE. Directors can invest.
- **EIS** — 30% relief and larger caps; for the round after SEIS.
- For an equity raise: be a Ltd, then apply for **SEIS advance assurance** (4-6 weeks)
  before approaching investors. Verify every limit against gov.uk.

## Important caveats
- **Innovate UK Smart Grants are paused** for FY 2025/26 — do not build a plan around them.
  The Growth Catalyst pilot is the nearest replacement.
- Programme windows open and close — always confirm the current eligibility window.
- A grant is not free money: match-funding, reporting, and time costs are real. Premortem
  any funding application that will take more than a few hours of effort.

## Method
1. Identify the venture's phase, legal structure, and the founder's region.
2. Shortlist 2-3 programmes — national and regional — that genuinely fit.
3. For each: eligibility, amount, effort, and the realistic timeline.
4. Recommend the single best next step — usually the free support before the money.
