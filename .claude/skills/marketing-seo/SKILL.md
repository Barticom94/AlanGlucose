# Marketing — SEO

SEO is a compounding, low-cost channel — and a slow one. It is the right long-term play for
a bootstrapped venture and the wrong launch channel. Set expectations honestly: 6-12 months
to meaningful traffic. For research, spawn the `seo-researcher` subagent.

## The three layers
1. **Technical** — the site is crawlable, fast, mobile-first, and cleanly structured.
   Most hosted platforms handle this; still check Core Web Vitals.
2. **Content** — pages that answer real search intent better than the current top results.
3. **Authority** — links and brand signals earned over time. Do not buy links.

## Method
1. Keyword research — group by intent (informational / commercial / transactional).
   Prioritise long-tail terms a new domain can realistically rank for.
2. Map keywords to pages — product, category, and content pages each serve different intent.
3. Write for the searcher first, the engine second. Match or beat the depth of what ranks now.
4. Build topical clusters: a pillar page plus supporting articles that link to it.
5. Track rankings, organic sessions, and — most importantly — organic conversions.

## UK specifics
- Target google.co.uk; UK English spelling; UK examples and pricing.
- If location matters, set up a Google Business Profile and target local search.

## Honest framing
- SEO will not deliver the first 10 customers. Use paid ads or direct outreach for launch.
- AI search and answer engines are changing discovery — structure content to be quotable,
  with clear answers and good headings.
- One genuinely useful page beats ten thin ones. Google and readers both punish thin content.
