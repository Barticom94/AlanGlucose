# UK & Yorkshire Funding

Yorkshire support is dense and underused. Match the venture's stage and structure to the
right programme. Full directory with eligibility and links: `@docs/UK-FUNDING.md`.

## Match funding to phase
- **Phase 0-1 (validating):** mostly free support — AD:VENTURE Business Manager, NatWest
  Accelerator Leeds, Barclays Eagle Labs Leeds, Help to Grow: Management.
- **Phase 2 (MVP):** Start Up Loans (up to £25k personal, fixed 6% APR, 12 months free
  mentoring); the AD:VENTURE growth grant (£1,500-£10,000, 50% match).
- **Phase 3-4 (traction/growth):** Northern Powerhouse Investment Fund II loans
  (£25k-£100k via Business Enterprise Fund; larger amounts via Mercia); the Mayor's Big
  Ideas Challenge.
- **Phase 5 (scale/raise):** SEIS/EIS equity, angel groups, SEIS funds.

## SEIS / EIS at a glance
- **SEIS** — 50% income tax relief for investors, £200k investor cap, £500k company cap;
  company under 3 years trading, gross assets ≤ £350k, under 25 FTE. Directors can invest.
- **EIS** — 30% relief and larger caps; for the round after SEIS.
- For an equity raise: be a Ltd, then apply for **SEIS advance assurance** (4-6 weeks)
  before approaching investors. Verify every limit against gov.uk.

## Important caveats
- **Innovate UK Smart Grants are paused** for FY 2025/26 — do not build a plan around them.
  The Growth Catalyst pilot is the nearest replacement.
- Yorkshire programme windows open and close — always confirm the current eligibility window.
- A grant is not free money: match-funding, reporting, and time costs are real. Premortem
  any funding application that will take more than a few hours of effort.

## Method
1. Identify the venture's phase and legal structure.
2. Shortlist 2-3 programmes from `@docs/UK-FUNDING.md` that genuinely fit.
3. For each: eligibility, amount, effort, and the realistic timeline.
4. Recommend the single best next step — usually the free support before the money.
