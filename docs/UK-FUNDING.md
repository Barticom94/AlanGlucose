## How to use this

Match the venture's **phase** and **legal structure** to a programme. Free support before
paid money — and remember a grant is never free: match-funding, reporting, and time costs
are real. Premortem any application that will cost more than a few hours.

## Investor tax relief — SEIS / EIS / VCT

See `@docs/UK-LEGAL-TAX.md` for full figures. Headline: **SEIS** gives investors **50%**
income tax relief (£200k investor cap, £500k company cap) and is the most compelling pitch
a UK angel hears. A company must be a **Ltd** and should hold **SEIS advance assurance**
(4–6 weeks) before approaching investors.

## UK-wide programmes

### Start Up Loans
**£500–£25,000** personal loans (max £100k per business across up to 4 directors), fixed
**6% APR**, 1–5 year term, with **12 months of free mentoring**. UK resident, 18+, business
under 36 months old. Available across the whole UK.
→ startuploans.co.uk

### Growth Catalyst (Innovate UK)
A pilot replacing the paused Smart Grants — **£25,000–£50,000**, **100% grant**, for
critical-technology early-stage work. UK-registered, under 50 FTE, no prior Innovate UK grant.
→ apply-for-innovation-funding.service.gov.uk

### Help to Grow: Management
A 12-week leadership course, around 90% government-funded (the founder pays roughly £750).
For SME senior leaders; delivered by business schools across the UK.
→ search "Help to Grow Management" on gov.uk

## Regional & local support

Every part of the UK has regional and local business support — it varies by where you are.
Find yours; do not assume it does not exist.

- **England — Growth Hubs.** Every region and sub-region has a Growth Hub: free advice,
  signposting, workshops, and often small grants. Combined Authorities and elected Mayors
  run their own funds and grant challenges; local councils run small local grants.
- **British Business Bank regional funds.** The BBB runs regional investment funds offering
  SME loans and equity — for example the Northern Powerhouse Investment Fund and the
  Midlands Engine Investment Fund. Check whether one covers your region.
- **Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland.** Scotland — Business Gateway and Scottish
  Enterprise. Wales — Business Wales. Northern Ireland — Invest NI. Each is the national
  equivalent of England's Growth Hub network.
- **Bank-run accelerators.** NatWest Accelerator and Barclays Eagle Labs run free
  programmes — coaching, events, workspace, pitch days — in many UK cities, open to any
  founder, not just that bank's customers.

**Find your region's programmes:** the gov.uk Business Finance Support finder
(gov.uk/business-finance-support) and your local Growth Hub. The `uk-funding` skill will
research the current programmes for the founder's specific region on request.

## Important caveat

**Innovate UK Smart Grants are paused for FY 2025/26.** Do not build a plan around them.
Use the Growth Catalyst pilot or targeted sector competitions on the Innovation Funding
Service instead.

## Suggested approach order (for an equity-route venture)

Free regional support and validation first → regional investment funds (loans, then equity)
→ angel groups → SEIS funds (e.g. Jenson, SFC Capital). Money follows evidence, not the
other way round.
