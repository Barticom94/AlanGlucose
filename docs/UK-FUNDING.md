## How to use this

Match the venture's **phase** and **legal structure** to a programme. Free support before
paid money — and remember a grant is never free: match-funding, reporting, and time costs
are real. Premortem any application that will cost more than a few hours.

## Investor tax relief — SEIS / EIS / VCT

See `@docs/UK-LEGAL-TAX.md` for full figures. Headline: **SEIS** gives investors **50%**
income tax relief (£200k investor cap, £500k company cap) and is the most compelling pitch
a UK angel hears. A company must be a **Ltd** and should hold **SEIS advance assurance**
(4–6 weeks) before approaching investors.

## Yorkshire & Leeds City Region programmes

### AD:VENTURE
Free Business Manager, workshops, and a **£1,500–£10,000** growth grant (50% match).
Eligibility: trading **under 3 years**; based in Bradford, Calderdale, Kirklees, Leeds, or
Wakefield; B2B, or B2C with **over 50% out-of-region** turnover; ambition to reach the VAT
threshold within 2 years.
→ ad-venture.org.uk · adventure@leeds.gov.uk · 0113 535 1199

### West Yorkshire Combined Authority Business Growth Hub
"No wrong door" access to finance, skills, workspace, and innovation support. Launched
**3 March 2026** by Mayor Tracy Brabin. West Yorkshire businesses.
→ westyorks-ca.gov.uk/business/growth-hub/

### Mayor's Big Ideas Challenge
Grants of **£20,000–£100,000** for target sectors; young entrepreneur awards under £600
(ages 16–26). West Yorkshire; periodic rounds.
→ westyorks-ca.gov.uk

### Start Up Loans
**£500–£25,000** personal loans (max £100k per business across up to 4 directors), fixed
**6% APR**, 1–5 year term, with **12 months of free mentoring**. UK resident, 18+, business
under 36 months old.
→ startuploans.co.uk

### Northern Powerhouse Investment Fund II
£660m total. Yorkshire delivery: **£25k–£100k loans** via Business Enterprise Fund;
**£100k–£2m loans** via Mercia Debt; **up to £5m equity** via Mercia Equity Finance.
Northern England SMEs.
→ northernpowerhouseinvestmentfund.co.uk

### NatWest Accelerator (Leeds)
Free coaching, events, and pitch days — open to any UK entrepreneur, not just NatWest
customers.
→ search Eventbrite for "Leeds NatWest Accelerator"

### Barclays Eagle Labs (Leeds)
Co-working, events, and mentoring; access to national programmes. At Avenue HQ, 10–12 East
Parade, Leeds.
→ labs.uk.barclays

### York & North Yorkshire Growth Hub
Free support, the Get Funded service, and the Great Ideas £2.5m innovation fund (grants up
to £60,000). York and North Yorkshire.
→ ynygrowthhub.com

### HEY Growing Places Fund
Grants of **£5,000–£250,000** plus loans of **£75,000–£250,000**. Hull & East Yorkshire;
profitable and project-ready businesses.
→ heybusinessgrowthskillshub.com

### Growth Catalyst (Innovate UK)
A pilot replacing the paused Smart Grants — **£25,000–£50,000**, **100% grant**, for
critical-technology early-stage work. UK-registered, under 50 FTE, no prior Innovate UK grant.
→ apply-for-innovation-funding.service.gov.uk

### Help to Grow: Management
A 12-week course, 90% government-funded (the founder pays £750). For SME senior leaders.
→ leedsbeckett.ac.uk/help-to-grow

## Important caveat

**Innovate UK Smart Grants are paused for FY 2025/26.** Do not build a plan around them.
Use the Growth Catalyst pilot or targeted sector competitions on the Innovation Funding
Service instead.

## Suggested approach order (for an equity-route venture)

AD:VENTURE network and free support → NPIF II equity (via Mercia) → angel groups → SEIS
funds (e.g. Jenson, SFC Capital). Free support and validation first; money follows evidence.
