## VAT

- **Registration threshold: £90,000** of VAT-taxable turnover on a rolling 12-month basis
  (unchanged at Spring Statement 2026). **Deregistration threshold: £88,000.**
- **Standard rate: 20%.**
- **Flat Rate Scheme** available if VAT-taxable turnover is **≤ £150,000** (excl. VAT) —
  can reduce admin for a small business.
- Voluntary registration below the threshold is allowed and is sometimes worthwhile to
  reclaim input VAT.
- For a B2C ecommerce business, crossing the threshold is a **margin cliff**: you absorb the
  20% or raise prices. Model it well before you approach £90k.

## Corporation Tax (limited companies, 2026/27)

- **19%** small-profits rate on profits up to **£50,000**.
- **25%** main rate on profits of **£250,000** and above.
- **Marginal relief** applies between £50,000 and £250,000.

## Making Tax Digital for Income Tax (MTD-IT)

- Mandatory from **6 April 2026** for self-employed people and landlords with qualifying
  income **over £50,000** (assessed on 2024–25).
- Threshold drops to **over £30,000 from April 2027**, then **over £20,000 from April 2028**.
- Requires MTD-compatible software and quarterly updates. Check the gov.uk recognised-software
  list before choosing a package.

## SEIS — Seed Enterprise Investment Scheme

(Post-April 2025 limits, unchanged for 2026.)
- **50%** income tax relief for investors.
- **£200,000** investor cap; **£500,000** company lifetime cap.
- Company must be **under 3 years** trading, gross assets **≤ £350,000**, **under 25**
  full-time-equivalent employees.
- **Directors can invest** under SEIS.
- For an equity raise, apply for **SEIS advance assurance** — allow roughly **4–6 weeks**.

## EIS — Enterprise Investment Scheme (from 6 April 2026)

- **30%** income tax relief; **£1m** investor cap (**£2m** for knowledge-intensive companies).
- Company gross-asset test: **≤ £30m before / ≤ £35m after** the investment.
- Annual raise limit: **£10m** (**£20m** KIC). Lifetime: **£24m** (**£40m** KIC).

## VCT — Venture Capital Trusts

- Income tax relief **drops from 30% to 20% from 6 April 2026.**

## Companies House fees (from 1 February 2026)

- **Digital incorporation: £100** (previously £50).
- **Confirmation statement: £50** digital (previously £34).
- **Voluntary strike-off: £13** digital (previously £33).

## Identity verification

- Mandatory for **new directors and PSCs from 18 November 2025**.
- **Existing** officers must verify **by 18 November 2026**.

## EORI numbers

- Free to apply at **gov.uk/eori/apply-for-eori**.
- Required to move goods between Great Britain and any other country.
- A separate **XI EORI** is needed for Northern Ireland under the Windsor Framework.

## Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 (distance selling)

- **14-day cooling-off period** for online sales — the customer may change their mind. It
  starts when they receive the goods.
- If you do not inform the customer of their cancellation rights, the period **extends up
  to 12 months**.
- Refunds — including the **basic delivery cost** — within **14 days** of receiving the
  goods back (or proof of return).

## Consumer Rights Act 2015

- Goods must be of **satisfactory quality, fit for purpose, and as described**.
- A faulty item carries a **30-day short-term right to reject** for a full refund.

## UK GDPR & PECR

- **PECR fines now match UK GDPR levels** — up to **£17.5m or 4% of global turnover** —
  raised by the **Data (Use and Access) Act 2025**.
- Cookie consent banners must offer **equal-prominence Accept and Reject** options.
- A **soft opt-in** permits email marketing to **existing customers** for **similar
  products**, provided a clear opt-out was offered at the point of sale and in every message.
- For non-customers, explicit consent is required — no pre-ticked boxes.

## Sector regulators to watch

Flag any exposure to the **FCA** (financial services), **MHRA** (medicines, medical
devices, cosmetics claims), **Ofcom** (communications), **ICO** (data), and **Trading
Standards / ASA** (advertising claims). If a venture touches a regulator's jurisdiction,
verify the specific requirements before launch.
