# legal/

Customer-facing legal documents and supplier agreements.

## Contents
- `terms-and-conditions.md` — website and sales terms (template).
- `privacy-policy.md` — UK GDPR / PECR privacy policy (template).
- `returns-policy.md` — returns and refunds, per UK consumer law (template).
- `supplier-contracts/` — signed or draft supplier agreements.

## Important
These are **templates and starting points, not finished legal documents.** UK consumer law
(Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, Consumer Rights Act 2015) and data law (UK GDPR,
PECR) are not optional — see `@docs/UK-LEGAL-TAX.md`. Before publishing anything from this
folder, have it reviewed by a UK solicitor or use a reputable UK legal-template service.
This folder is information, not legal advice.
