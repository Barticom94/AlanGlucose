# Pack — Ecommerce

Selling through an online store: a founder's own site (Shopify or similar), and/or a
marketplace (Amazon UK, Etsy, eBay, TikTok Shop). This pack adds the skills and reference
knowledge an ecommerce venture needs on top of the core brain — it does not replace the core
skills (financial modelling, UK tax, legal structure, marketing, and so on all still apply).

## When the brain installs this pack
When the intake (`state/business-brief.md`, section 1.2 — "what you'll actually sell") says
the venture sells a physical or digital product to the end customer through an online store
or marketplace, rather than delivering a service or software. Install it at Phase 1 at the
earliest, when a marketplace-vs-store comparison first becomes useful research — but do not
act on anything in it (register, list, build, or pay for a platform) before the Phase 1 gate:
≥ 3 real commitments (`AGENTS.md`).

## Skills in this pack
- **`ecom-shopify`** — build and run a Shopify store: plan, theme, products, payments,
  policies, and the funnel from product page to checkout.
- **`ecom-marketplaces-uk`** — sell via Amazon UK, Etsy, eBay, and TikTok Shop: fee
  structures, when a marketplace beats an own store (and when it does not), the Buy Box,
  reviews, and the UK consumer-law and VAT points that bite marketplace sellers.
- **`ecom-conversion-analytics`** — store measurement and conversion: GA4 / platform
  analytics setup, the five metrics that matter (sessions, conversion rate, AOV, CAC,
  returning-customer rate), product-page and checkout CRO, and honest A/B testing at small
  volumes.

## Ops reference
- **`docs/ECOM-OPS.md`** — the long-form ecommerce operations reference: UK carrier rates,
  selling into the EU post-Brexit, bookkeeping software, the no-code tool stack, and the
  operational layer (customer service, inventory, returns, suppliers) once the venture is
  live. Loaded on demand via `@docs/ECOM-OPS.md` — not auto-loaded. The inventory, returns
  and supplier sections assume the physical-goods pack is also installed.

## Packs are additive
A venture can use more than one pack — for example, a founder who sells a physical product
(`packs/physical/`) through their own Shopify store and on Amazon uses `ecom-shopify` and
`ecom-marketplaces-uk` from this pack, `fulfilment-shipping-uk` and `ops-inventory` from the
physical-goods pack, and every core skill besides. Only install the skills a venture actually
needs — an unused skill adds noise, not safety.
