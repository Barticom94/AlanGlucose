# Pack: Physical

Physical products, premises, and in-person or local services. Everything a venture needs once
part of the value it delivers is physical or happens in a specific place — sourcing goods,
holding stock, trading from a shop or stall, or turning up to do a job in person.

## When the brain installs this pack

`business-intake` installs the packs that fit the venture during intake, based on section 1.2
of the intake framework — "What you'll actually sell": a physical product, a digital product,
a service, or a marketplace — read alongside how the venture reaches the customer. This pack
is installed when the answer involves any of:

- a physical product the founder sources, makes, or stocks (goods that get shipped or handed over)
- a shop, unit, market stall, van, or other fixed or mobile premises
- a service delivered in person or at a specific location, rather than purely online

It can install alongside another pack — see "Packs are additive" below.

Installed at Phase 1 at the earliest, as research. Nothing in this pack is acted on before
the Phase 1 gate — ≥ 3 real commitments (`AGENTS.md`). Costing a lease, quoting a carrier, and
pricing a licence are validation-safe; signing a lease, ordering stock, paying a licence fee,
or giving a personal guarantee are not.

## Skills in this pack

- **`ops-suppliers`** — find, vet, and manage suppliers; due diligence, sampling, and the
  supplier scorecard.
- **`ops-inventory`** — manage stock: tooling by stage, reorder points, stock turn, and days
  of cover.
- **`ops-returns`** — set up and run returns and refunds under UK consumer law.
- **`fulfilment-shipping-uk`** — plan UK and EU order fulfilment: carriers, shipping cost,
  packaging, and post-Brexit EU sales.
- **`premises-licensing-uk`** — leases, business rates and Small Business Rate Relief,
  planning use classes, licences (food, alcohol, street trading, music), the Food Hygiene
  Rating Scheme, health and safety, and public liability insurance.

## Ops document

- **`docs/PHYSICAL-OPS.md`** — the physical operating loop (source → stock → sell → ship or
  serve → returns), premises basics, and the UK rules that bite: product safety and UKCA
  marking, labelling, food hygiene, and packaging waste (pEPR) obligations. Load on demand via
  `@docs/PHYSICAL-OPS.md` — it is not auto-loaded at session start.

## Packs are additive

A venture can install more than one pack — a shop that also sells online needs both
`physical` and `ecommerce`; a software tool sold with an in-person installation visit could
need both `saas` and `physical`. Installing this pack does not remove or replace another; each
pack only adds skills and reference docs relevant to that part of the venture.
