# Operations — Returns & Refunds

For a physical venture, returns are a legal obligation with hard deadlines — but which
obligation depends on where the sale happened. Get the policy right before launch.

## How to use this skill
1. Confirm the gate first — check `state/progress.md`. Below Phase 2, draft the policy as
   research; do not publish it or promise it to a customer yet.
2. Identify which sale type applies — on-premises, distance, or off-premises (below) — since
   the legal minimum differs by type.
3. Write the returns policy in plain English against the correct legal minimum for that sale
   type, then decide where to exceed it because it is cheap to do so.
4. Decide and cost the returns route — pre-paid label, customer-paid, or in-person exchange.
5. Set restocking criteria — what can be resold, and what cannot.
6. Track the return rate and reasons; treat a high rate on one product as product feedback,
   not just a logistics problem.

## UK consumer law — the non-negotiables
- **Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013** — the 14-day cooling-off right applies to *distance*
  sales (online, phone, mail order) and *off-premises* sales (agreed in the customer's home or
  workplace), NOT to on-premises sales in a shop or from a stall. On an in-store sale there is
  no statutory right to a refund for a change of mind; any such policy is voluntary and should
  be priced as a cost, not assumed. Failing to inform the customer of cancellation rights
  extends the period by 12 months (gov.uk / Business Companion, "Consumer contracts: distance
  sales", verified Sep 2026).
- **Off-premises services** — if work is to start inside the 14-day period, get the customer's
  express request in writing, or the trader risks being unable to charge for work done if the
  customer cancels.
- Refunds must be issued within 14 days of getting the goods back (or proof of return), for
  sales where the cooling-off right applies. You must refund the basic delivery cost too.
- **Consumer Rights Act 2015** — goods must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose, and
  as described. A faulty item carries a 30-day short-term right to reject for a full refund.
See `@docs/UK-LEGAL-TAX.md` and the `legal/` folder. This is information, not legal advice.

## The process to build
1. A clear returns policy on the site, in plain English. Exceeding the legal minimum where
   it is cheap to do so builds trust.
2. A simple returns route — a pre-paid label (Royal Mail Tracked Returns, Evri Returns) or
   customer-paid. Decide and cost it in `state/financials.md`.
3. Restocking criteria — what can be resold, and what cannot.
4. Fast refunds — within the legal window, ideally faster. Slow refunds generate complaints
   and card chargebacks.

## The number
Track the return rate and the reasons. A high return rate on one product is product
feedback — sizing, description, or quality. Fix the cause, not just the logistics.

## Output
Update `state/financials.md` with the return rate and the refund/replacement cost it drives.
Log the returns-policy decision in `state/decisions_log.md`.
