# Operations — Suppliers

A supplier is a dependency. A bad one quietly caps the venture's quality, margin, and
delivery promise. Vet before you commit.

## How to use this skill
1. Confirm the gate first — check `state/progress.md`. Below Phase 2, treat sourcing as
   research: shortlist and get quotes, but do not commit to a MOQ or place a paid order.
2. Identify at least two candidate suppliers for the input — never plan around a single
   source for anything critical.
3. Run due diligence on each candidate (below) before any volume conversation.
4. Order a sample and score it before committing to anything beyond a sample quantity.
5. Negotiate MOQ, price, payment terms, and defect/return handling, and get the terms in
   writing.
6. Build the true landed cost — unit + freight + duty + handling — into `state/financials.md`.
7. Log the supplier choice, and any single-source dependency, before moving on.

## Due diligence on a new supplier
- For UK suppliers, run the Companies House MCP: incorporation date, filed accounts,
  directors, charges. A young company with no accounts and floating charges is a risk worth
  knowing about.
- Ask for references — and actually call them.
- Order a sample before any volume commitment. Always.

## The supplier scorecard
Rate and re-rate each supplier on:
- **Lead time** — and its reliability, not just the quoted figure.
- **MOQ** — minimum order quantity; lower is better for a bootstrap's cash.
- **Defect rate** — track it from real deliveries.
- **Payment terms** — upfront vs 30 days makes a real difference to working capital.
- **Communication** — slow or evasive now is worse under pressure later.
- **Price** — last. The cheapest supplier that misses lead times is not the cheapest.

## Reducing supplier risk
1. Avoid a single point of failure on a critical input — line up a known second source.
2. Get the key terms in writing — price, lead time, defect handling, who pays return freight.
3. Negotiate the MOQ down early; raise volume once demand is proven.
4. Build the true landed cost — unit + freight + duty + handling — into `state/financials.md`.

## The discipline
Review suppliers on the scorecard quarterly. A supplier that was fine at launch can drift —
catch it before a customer does.

## Output
Update `state/financials.md` with the landed cost and supplier terms. Log the supplier-choice
decision in `state/decisions_log.md`. If the venture has no second source for a critical
input, log that single-source dependency in `state/risks.md`.
