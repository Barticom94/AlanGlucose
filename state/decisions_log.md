# Decisions Log

> Append-only. Newest entry at the top. One entry per real decision. Never edit an old
> entry — if a decision is reversed, add a new entry that supersedes it.

## How to log a decision
Copy this shape, newest at the top:

> **### [YYYY-MM-DD] Short decision title**
> - **Decision:** what was decided
> - **Context:** what prompted it
> - **Alternatives considered:** what else was on the table
> - **Why:** the reason
> - **Supersedes:** an earlier entry, if any (`—` when none)
> - **Whose:** `founder — "<the sentence in which they chose>"` or `brain — recommendation;
>   founder's decision not given`. The founder's line is a quotation that names the choice
>   ("ok fair on the company thing", "log it that I'm proceeding without checking it"); an
>   acknowledgement or a topic close — "ok", "fine", "leave it then", "yeah I know", "not
>   sure" — is not one, and no entry is written on it: a hypothetical explored and dropped, or
>   a recommendation the founder has not answered, goes to `state/active_context.md` Open
>   questions as "<the recommendation> — founder's decision not given", and comes here on the
>   day they choose.
